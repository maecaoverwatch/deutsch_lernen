# German A1: Welcher, Welche, Welches
# Question Words Across Nominativ, Akkusativ, and Dativ

## 1. What does *welcher* mean?

**Welcher, welche, welches** mean **which** when asking about a specific person or thing.

The form changes depending on the noun's **gender**, whether it is **singular or plural**, and its **case**.

---

## 2. Quick reminder: Nominativ, Akkusativ, and Dativ

| Case | Function | Question |
|---|---|---|
| **Nominativ** | The subject of the sentence | Wer? Was? — Who? What? |
| **Akkusativ** | The direct object | Wen? Was? — Whom? What? |
| **Dativ** | The indirect object; also used after certain verbs and prepositions | Wem? — To whom? |

Examples:

- **Der Mann** kauft einen Apfel. — The man buys an apple. *(Nominativ)*
- Ich sehe **den Mann**. — I see the man. *(Akkusativ)*
- Ich gebe **dem Mann** einen Apfel. — I give the man an apple. *(Dativ)*

Notice how *der Mann* changes to *den Mann* and *dem Mann*. The forms of **welcher** also change according to case.

---

## 3. Welcher in Nominativ

Use these forms when the noun is the **subject**.

| Gender / number | Form | Example | English |
|---|---|---|---|
| Masculine | **welcher** | Welcher Mann kommt? | Which man is coming? |
| Feminine | **welche** | Welche Frau kommt? | Which woman is coming? |
| Neuter | **welches** | Welches Kind kommt? | Which child is coming? |
| Plural | **welche** | Welche Kinder kommen? | Which children are coming? |

More examples:

- **Welcher** Bus fährt nach Nairobi? — Which bus goes to Nairobi?
- **Welche** Tasche gehört dir? — Which bag belongs to you?
- **Welches** Buch ist interessant? — Which book is interesting?

---

## 4. Welcher in Akkusativ

Use these forms when the noun is the **direct object**.

| Gender / number | Form | Example | English |
|---|---|---|---|
| Masculine | **welchen** | Welchen Mann siehst du? | Which man do you see? |
| Feminine | **welche** | Welche Frau siehst du? | Which woman do you see? |
| Neuter | **welches** | Welches Kind siehst du? | Which child do you see? |
| Plural | **welche** | Welche Kinder siehst du? | Which children do you see? |

More examples:

- **Welchen** Film schaust du? — Which film are you watching?
- **Welche** Blume kaufst du? — Which flower are you buying?
- **Welches** Auto möchtest du? — Which car would you like?

**Important:** In the masculine Akkusativ, *welcher* changes to **welchen**.

Compare:
- Nominativ: **Welcher Mann** kommt?
- Akkusativ: **Welchen Mann** siehst du?

---

## 5. Welcher in Dativ

Use these forms when the noun is in the **Dativ**. The Dativ is used after certain verbs and prepositions, including **mit** (with).

| Gender / number | Form | Example | English |
|---|---|---|---|
| Masculine | **welchem** | Mit welchem Mann sprichst du? | Which man are you speaking with? |
| Feminine | **welcher** | Mit welcher Frau sprichst du? | Which woman are you speaking with? |
| Neuter | **welchem** | Mit welchem Kind sprichst du? | Which child are you speaking with? |
| Plural | **welchen** | Mit welchen Kindern sprichst du? | Which children are you speaking with? |

More examples:

- **Mit welchem** Bus fährst du? — Which bus are you taking?
- **Mit welcher** Freundin sprichst du? — Which female friend are you speaking with?
- **Mit welchem** Lehrer sprichst du? — Which teacher are you speaking with?
- **Mit welchen** Freunden gehst du? — Which friends are you going with?

### Dativ plural noun ending

Dative plural nouns often add **-n** if they do not already end in **-n** or **-s**.

- die Kinder → mit welchen **Kindern**
- die Freunde → mit welchen **Freunden**

## 6. Complete table to memorize

| Case | Masculine | Feminine | Neuter | Plural |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Nominativ** | welcher | welche | welches | welche |
| **Akkusativ** | welchen | welche | welches | welche |
| **Dativ** | welchem | welcher | welchem | welchen |

### Memory trick: focus on the endings

| Case | Masculine | Feminine | Neuter | Plural |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Nominativ | **-er** | **-e** | **-es** | **-e** |
| Akkusativ | **-en** | **-e** | **-es** | **-e** |
| Dativ | **-em** | **-er** | **-em** | **-en** |

The correct form depends on both **case** and **gender/number**.
