## 3. Describing Light, Dark, and Bright Colors

| German | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| hell | light | hellblau — light blue |
| dunkel | dark | dunkelgrün — dark green |
| blass | pale | blassrosa — pale pink |
| kräftig | strong/vivid | kräftiges Rot — vivid red |
| leuchtend | bright/vibrant | leuchtendes Gelb — bright yellow |
| matt | matte/dull | mattes Schwarz — matte black |
| glänzend | shiny | glänzendes Gold — shiny gold |

### Combining color words

- **dunkel + rot = dunkelrot** — dark red
- **Himmel + blau = himmelblau** — sky blue
- **Oliv + grün = olivgrün** — olive green

Many German color names are compound words and are written as one word.

---

## 4. Using Colors in Sentences

A simple sentence pattern is:

**Noun + form of _sein_ + color.**

| German | English |
|---|---|
| Das Auto ist rot. | The car is red. |
| Das Haus ist weiß. | The house is white. |
| Die Tasche ist schwarz. | The bag is black. |
| Der Tisch ist braun. | The table is brown. |
| Die Blume ist gelb. | The flower is yellow. |
| Das Kleid ist blau. | The dress is blue. |
| Der Baum ist grün. | The tree is green. |
| Die Wand ist grau. | The wall is grey. |

In these sentences, the color comes after **ist**, so it does not take an adjective ending.

---

## 5. Asking About Colors

| German | English |
|---|---|
| Welche Farbe ist das? | What color is that? |
| Welche Farbe hat das Auto? | What color is the car? |
| Was ist deine Lieblingsfarbe? | What is your favorite color? |
| Meine Lieblingsfarbe ist Grün. | My favorite color is green. |
| Welche Farben magst du? | Which colors do you like? |
| Ich mag Blau und Grün. | I like blue and green. |

### Useful vocabulary

- **die Farbe** — the color
- **die Farben** — the colors
- **die Lieblingsfarbe** — the favorite color
- **Welche** — which/what
- **mögen** — to like

---

## 6. Colors Before Nouns: A Small Grammar Note

When a color comes **before a noun**, its ending can change depending on the article, gender, and case.

Compare:

| German | English |
|---|---|
| Das Auto ist rot. | The car is red. |
| ein rotes Auto | a red car |
| der rote Ball | the red ball |
| eine rote Tasche | a red bag |

For now, focus on learning the color words and using them in simple sentences. Adjective endings can be studied separately.

## 9. Quick Revision

- **die Farbe** = the color
- **die Farben** = the colors
- **rot** = red
- **blau** = blue
- **grün** = green
- **gelb** = yellow
- **schwarz** = black
- **weiß** = white
- **hell** = light
- **dunkel** = dark
- **Welche Farbe hat das Auto?** = What color is the car?
- **Meine Lieblingsfarbe ist Blau.** = My favorite color is blue.
- **Das Auto ist rot.** = The car is red.

**Study tip:** Say the colors aloud, point to objects around you, and describe them in German: *Der Tisch ist braun. Die Tasche ist schwarz.*
