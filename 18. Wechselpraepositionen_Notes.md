## 1. What are Wechselpräpositionen?

**Wechselpräpositionen** are German *two-way prepositions*. They can be used with either **Akkusativ** or **Dativ**.

The case depends on what the sentence means:

- **Akkusativ** is commonly used when the preposition describes a destination or a change to a new location. Ask **Wohin?** (Where to?).
- **Dativ** is commonly used when the preposition describes an existing location or position. Ask **Wo?** (Where?).

The verb alone does not decide the case. Look at the meaning of the whole sentence.

## 2. The nine Wechselpräpositionen

| German | Common English meaning |
|---|---|
| an | at, on (often touching a vertical surface) |
| auf | on, onto |
| hinter | behind |
| in | in, into |
| neben | next to, beside |
| über | above, over |
| unter | under, below |
| vor | in front of, before |
| zwischen | between |

The exact English translation depends on context.

## 3. The main rule: Wohin? vs. Wo?

### Akkusativ — Wohin? (Where to?)

Use Akkusativ when the sentence describes a destination or a change of location.

- Ich gehe **in die Küche**. — I go into the kitchen.
- Ich lege das Buch **auf den Tisch**. — I put the book onto the table.
- Ich hänge das Bild **an die Wand**. — I hang the picture on the wall.

### Dativ — Wo? (Where?)

Use Dativ when the sentence describes where a person or thing is located.

- Ich bin **in der Küche**. — I am in the kitchen.
- Das Buch liegt **auf dem Tisch**. — The book is lying on the table.
- Das Bild hängt **an der Wand**. — The picture is hanging on the wall.

### Quick reminder

| Question | Meaning | Case |
|---|---|---|
| **Wohin?** | Where to? Destination/change of location | Akkusativ |
| **Wo?** | Where? Existing location/position | Dativ |

## 4. Sentence structures

These are useful basic patterns, not the only possible German word orders.

### Akkusativ
**Subject + verb + Wechselpräposition + Akkusativ article + noun**

Examples:
- Ich gehe **in die Schule**. — I go to/into the school.
- Ich lege das Buch **auf den Tisch**. — I put the book onto the table.
- Ich stelle die Flasche **neben den Kühlschrank**. — I put the bottle next to the refrigerator.

### Dativ
**Subject + verb + Wechselpräposition + Dativ article + noun**

Examples:
- Ich bin **in der Schule**. — I am at school.
- Das Buch liegt **auf dem Tisch**. — The book is lying on the table.
- Die Flasche steht **neben dem Kühlschrank**. — The bottle is standing next to the refrigerator.

## 5. Articles in Akkusativ and Dativ

### Definite articles: der, die, das

| Gender/number | Nominativ | Akkusativ | Dativ |
|---|---|---|---|
| Masculine | der | **den** | **dem** |
| Feminine | die | **die** | **der** |
| Neuter | das | **das** | **dem** |
| Plural | die | **die** | **den** |

With Dativ plural, the noun often adds **-n** if it does not already end in -n or -s.

Examples:
- auf **den Tischen** — on the tables
- zwischen **den Häusern** — between the houses

### Indefinite articles: ein, eine

| Gender | Akkusativ | Dativ |
|---|---|---|
| Masculine | einen | einem |
| Feminine | eine | einer |
| Neuter | ein | einem |

Examples:
- Ich gehe in **einen Park**. — I go into a park.
- Ich bin in **einem Park**. — I am in a park.
- Ich lege es auf **eine Bank**. — I put it onto a bench.
- Es liegt auf **einer Bank**. — It is lying on a bench.

| kommen | to come |

| laufen | to walk, run |
| rennen | to run |
| springen | to jump |
| fliegen | to fly |
| fallen | to fall |
| steigen | to climb, ascend |
| klettern | to climb |
| treten | to step |
| rollen | to roll |
| schieben | to push |
| ziehen | to pull |
| tragen | to carry |
| bringen | to bring |
| legen | to lay, put something down |
| stellen | to put something upright |
| setzen | to seat someone / sit someone down |
| hängen | to hang something up |
| stecken | to put or insert something |

## 7. Verbs commonly used for location or position

These verbs often describe a position, location, or state. With a Wechselpräposition describing that location, Dativ is commonly used.

| German verb | English meaning |
|---|---|
| sein | to be |
| bleiben | to stay, remain |
| wohnen | to live, reside |
| leben | to live |
| stehen | to stand, be standing |
| liegen | to lie, be lying |
| sitzen | to sit, be seated |
| hängen | to hang, be hanging |
| stecken | to be stuck or inserted |
| ruhen | to rest |
| schlafen | to sleep |
| warten | to wait |
| sich befinden | to be located |
| verweilen | to remain, linger |
| schweben | to hover, float |
| liegen bleiben | to remain lying |
| stehen bleiben | to stop / remain standing |
| sitzen bleiben | to remain seated |

Examples:
- Ich bin **in der Küche**. — I am in the kitchen.
- Ich wohne **in Nairobi**. — I live in Nairobi.
- Das Buch liegt **auf dem Tisch**. — The book is lying on the table.
- Ich sitze **auf dem Stuhl**. — I am sitting on the chair.

**Note:** These are useful verbs to learn with location phrases, not a fixed list of verbs that always take Dativ. Some can be used in sentences with other cases or meanings.

## 8. Important verb pairs

These pairs help you describe the difference between changing a position and describing a position that already exists.

| Change a position | Describe a position | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| legen | liegen | to lay/put down → to lie/be lying |
| stellen | stehen | to put upright → to stand/be standing |
| setzen / sich setzen | sitzen | to seat/sit down → to sit/be seated |
| sich hinlegen | liegen | to lie down → to be lying |
| sich hinstellen | stehen | to stand in a position → to be standing |
| sich hinsetzen | sitzen | to sit down → to be sitting |
| etwas aufhängen | hängen | to hang something up → to be hanging |

Examples:
- Ich **lege** das Buch auf **den Tisch**. — I put the book onto the table.
- Das Buch **liegt** auf **dem Tisch**. — The book is lying on the table.
- Ich **stelle** die Tasse auf **den Tisch**. — I put the cup onto the table.
- Die Tasse **steht** auf **dem Tisch**. — The cup is standing on the table.
- Ich **setze** mich auf **den Stuhl**. — I sit down on the chair.
- Ich **sitze** auf **dem Stuhl**. — I am sitting on the chair.
- Ich **hänge** das Bild an **die Wand**. — I hang the picture on the wall.
- Das Bild **hängt** an **der Wand**. — The picture is hanging on the wall.

## 9. Compare Akkusativ and Dativ sentences

| Akkusativ: destination/change | Dativ: location/position |
|---|---|
| Ich gehe in **die Küche**. | Ich bin in **der Küche**. |
| I go into the kitchen. | I am in the kitchen. |
| Ich laufe in **den Park**. | Ich laufe in **dem Park** / **im Park**. |
| I run into the park. | I run in the park. |
| Ich lege das Buch auf **den Tisch**. | Das Buch liegt auf **dem Tisch**. |
| I put the book onto the table. | The book is lying on the table. |
| Ich stelle die Flasche neben **den Kühlschrank**. | Die Flasche steht neben **dem Kühlschrank**. |
| I put the bottle next to the refrigerator. | The bottle is standing next to the refrigerator. |
| Ich hänge das Bild an **die Wand**. | Das Bild hängt an **der Wand**. |
| I hang the picture on the wall. | The picture is hanging on the wall. |

Common contractions:
- **in dem = im**
- **an dem = am**
- **in das = ins**
- **an das = ans**

For example: *Ich bin im Park* = *Ich bin in dem Park.*

## 10. Important warning: movement does not automatically mean Akkusativ

Do not use the rule “any movement = Akkusativ.” Ask whether the prepositional phrase tells you the destination or the location.

- **Ich laufe im Park.** — I run in the park. **Dativ**: the park is where the running happens.
- **Ich laufe in den Park.** — I run into the park. **Akkusativ**: the park is my destination.

A person can move while staying within the same location. In that case, a Wechselpräposition can still take Dativ:
- Ich tanze **in der Küche**. — I dance in the kitchen.
- Das Kind spielt **im Garten**. — The child plays in the garden.

A change of position can use Akkusativ even when the person is not travelling far:
- Ich setze mich **auf den Stuhl**. — I sit down on the chair.

## 11. Practice exercises

Fill in the correct article. Decide whether the sentence needs Akkusativ or Dativ.

1. Das Buch liegt auf ___ Tisch. *(der Tisch)*
2. Ich lege das Buch auf ___ Tisch. *(der Tisch)*
3. Die Frau ist in ___ Küche. *(die Küche)*
4. Die Frau geht in ___ Küche. *(die Küche)*
5. Die Flasche steht neben ___ Kühlschrank. *(der Kühlschrank)*
6. Ich stelle die Flasche neben ___ Kühlschrank. *(der Kühlschrank)*
7. Das Bild hängt an ___ Wand. *(die Wand)*
8. Ich hänge das Bild an ___ Wand. *(die Wand)*
9. Wir spielen in ___ Garten. *(der Garten — location)*
10. Wir gehen in ___ Garten. *(der Garten — destination)*

## 12. Answers

1. **dem** — Dativ: the book is located on the table.
2. **den** — Akkusativ: the book is being placed onto the table.
3. **der** — Dativ: the woman is in the kitchen.
4. **die** — Akkusativ: the kitchen is the destination.
5. **dem** — Dativ: the bottle is located next to the refrigerator.
6. **den** — Akkusativ: the bottle is being placed next to the refrigerator.
7. **der** — Dativ: the picture is already hanging on the wall.
8. **die** — Akkusativ: the picture is being hung onto the wall.
9. **dem** (often **im Garten**) — Dativ: the garden is where we are playing.
10. **den** — Akkusativ: the garden is our destination.

## Quick summary

- **Wechselpräpositionen:** an, auf, hinter, in, neben, über, unter, vor, zwischen.
- Ask **Wohin?** (Where to?) → usually **Akkusativ** for a destination/change of location.
- Ask **Wo?** (Where?) → **Dativ** for a location or position.
- Masculine: **den** (Akkusativ), **dem** (Dativ).
- Feminine: **die** (Akkusativ), **der** (Dativ).
- Neuter: **das** (Akkusativ), **dem** (Dativ).
- Plural: **die** (Akkusativ), **den** (Dativ).
- Learn the pairs **legen/liegen**, **stellen/stehen**, and **setzen/sitzen**.
- **Movement alone does not decide the case**; the meaning of the prepositional phrase does.
