# 🇩🇪 Tageszeiten — Times of the Day

| German              | English                    | Example        |
| ------------------- | -------------------------- | -------------- |
| **der Morgen**      | morning                    | am Morgen      |
| **der Vormittag**   | late morning / before noon | am Vormittag   |
| **der Mittag**      | midday/noon                | am Mittag      |
| **der Nachmittag**  | afternoon                  | am Nachmittag  |
| **der Abend**       | evening                    | am Abend       |
| **die Nacht**       | night                      | in der Nacht   |
| **die Mitternacht** | midnight                   | um Mitternacht |

## 🕐 Rough Time Guide

* 🌅 **der Morgen** → 5:00–10:00
* ☀️ **der Vormittag** → 10:00–12:00
* 🕛 **der Mittag** → around 12:00
* 🌤️ **der Nachmittag** → 12:00–18:00
* 🌇 **der Abend** → 18:00–22:00
* 🌙 **die Nacht** → 22:00–5:00

These times are approximate.

# 👋 Greetings

| German            | English            |
| ----------------- | ------------------ |
| **Guten Morgen!** | Good morning!      |
| **Guten Tag!**    | Good day! / Hello! |
| **Guten Abend!**  | Good evening!      |
| **Gute Nacht!**   | Good night!        |

### Important difference

**Guten Abend** → used when greeting someone in the evening.

> Guten Abend! Wie geht es Ihnen?

**Gute Nacht** → usually used when saying goodbye before someone goes to sleep.

> Gute Nacht! Schlaf gut!

# ⏰ Talking About When Something Happens

Use **am** with most parts of the day:

> **am Morgen** = in the morning
> **am Vormittag** = in the late morning
> **am Mittag** = at noon
> **am Nachmittag** = in the afternoon
> **am Abend** = in the evening

But:

> **in der Nacht** = at night
> **um Mitternacht** = at midnight

### Examples

> Ich arbeite **am Morgen**.
> I work in the morning.

> Ich lerne Deutsch **am Nachmittag**.
> I study German in the afternoon.

> Ich esse **am Abend**.
> I eat in the evening.

> Ich schlafe **in der Nacht**.
> I sleep at night.
