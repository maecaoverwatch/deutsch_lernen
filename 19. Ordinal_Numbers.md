## 1. What are ordinal numbers?

**Ordinalzahlen** are ordinal numbers. They tell us the order or position of something.

Examples:
- first
- second
- third
- fourth
- twenty-first

In German, ordinal numbers are often formed by adding **-te** or **-ste** to a cardinal number. When used before a noun, they behave like adjectives and their endings can change.

## 2. The basic endings: -te and -ste

### Numbers 1–19
Ordinal numbers from 1 to 19 usually use **-te**.

Examples:
- vier → vierte (fourth)
- zehn → zehnte (tenth)
- dreizehn → dreizehnte (thirteenth)

### Numbers 20 and above
Ordinal numbers from 20 onwards usually use **-ste**.

Examples:
- zwanzig → zwanzigste (twentieth)
- dreißig → dreißigste (thirtieth)
- vierzig → vierzigste (fortieth)

### Important forms to memorise
- 1st: **erste**
- 3rd: **dritte**
- 7th: **siebte**
- 8th: **achte**

These do not follow the simplest add-the-ending pattern exactly, so learn them as special forms.

## 6. Ordinal endings in sentences

The forms in the tables are basic forms. When ordinal numbers are used before nouns, they act like adjectives, so their endings can change according to the article, gender, number, and case.

Examples:
- **der erste Tag** — the first day
- **die erste Woche** — the first week
- **das erste Kind** — the first child
- **die ersten Tage** — the first days
- **am ersten Tag** — on the first day
- **am dritten Tag** — on the third day

Notice:
- **erste** in *der erste Tag*
- **ersten** in *am ersten Tag*

This happens because **am** is a contraction of **an dem**, and *dem* is Dativ masculine/neuter. The ordinal ending changes to **-en** in this phrase.

## 7. Ordinal numbers in dates

Ordinal numbers are commonly used for dates in German.

Examples:
- **Heute ist der erste Oktober.** — Today is the first of October.
- **Heute ist der einundzwanzigste Juni.** — Today is the twenty-first of June.
- **Ich habe am dritten Mai Geburtstag.** — My birthday is on the third of May.
- **Wir treffen uns am zehnten August.** — We are meeting on the tenth of August.

### A useful date pattern

- **Der + ordinal number + month** when stating the date:
  - Heute ist der fünfte März. — Today is the fifth of March.
- **Am + ordinal number ending in -en + month** when saying “on” a date:
  - am fünften März — on the fifth of March
  - am einundzwanzigsten Juni — on the twenty-first of June

## 10. Quick summary

- **1–19:** usually add **-te**.
- **20 and above:** usually add **-ste**.
- Memorise **erste, dritte, siebte, achte**.
- Ordinal numbers used before nouns behave like adjectives, so their endings can change.
- For dates, use **der erste Mai** to state the date and **am ersten Mai** to say “on the first of May.”
